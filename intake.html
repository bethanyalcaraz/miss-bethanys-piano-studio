05 — Tuition & Studio Agreement

  • Enrollment fee. A one-time $50 enrollment fee per student is due at registration to reserve your lesson time.
  • Monthly tuition. $160/month for 30-minute lessons or $225/month for 45-minute lessons.
  • Your reserved spot. Each family reserves a day of the week and a time. That spot is yours all year — the same lesson time, every week.
  • Same tuition every month. Tuition is the same each month — including shorter months like December and months with a fifth week.
  • School calendar. The studio follows the school calendar, so there are no lessons on school holidays. In months with a fifth week, there are no lessons that fifth week.
  • Make-up lessons. If I need to cancel a lesson, it will always be made up. Make-ups for lessons a student misses may be offered when time allows, but aren't guaranteed.
  • In-home lessons. Offered on Fridays only, with a monthly travel fee based on distance.
